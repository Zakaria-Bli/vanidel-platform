# 4.4 Participant Experience: Privacy & Regulations

Vanidel operates in full compliance with Algerian consumer and data privacy frameworks.

---

## 4.4.1 Accessing the Contest Regulations (_Règlement_)

Every campaign provides public access to its official contest regulations:

1. **In the Main Steps**: Step 2 or 3 of the contest page explicitly references the rules.
2. **Downloadable PDF File**: Contests configure an official, stamped bailiff regulation (_Règlement déposé_) available as a direct PDF download in both French and Arabic.
3. **Mandatory Acceptance Checkbox**: When submitting their entry, participants must check the box: _"J’accepte le règlement du concours."_ The label links directly to the PDF file.

### Regulatory Compliance Framework

In accordance with **Loi n° 18-07 du 10 juin 2018 relative à la protection des personnes physiques dans le traitement des données à caractère personnel**, modified and completed by **Loi n° 25-11**:

1. **Explicit Purpose Limitation**: Data collected (name, phone, wilaya, receipt image) is processed solely for contest administration, eligibility verification, winner selection, and winner announcement.
2. **Consent Versioning**: For every submission, the system records a cryptographic hash of the exact consent wording accepted by the participant, guaranteeing auditable proof of agreement.
3. **Receipt Image Isolation**: Receipt photographs are stored in a protected bucket and can never be downloaded or accessed without staff authentication.
4. **Data Retention Limits**: Data is retained only for the legally mandated contest duration plus auditing periods, configured in days in global settings.
5. **Participant Rights**: Participants may request access to, rectification of, or erasure of their personal information by contacting Vanidel's data protection delegate at `contact@vanidel.com`.
