# 4.3 Participant Experience: Viewing Winners & Archives

Transparency and public trust are fundamental to the Vanidel contest experience. The platform automatically displays drawn winners while protecting participant privacy.

---

## 4.3.1 Public Winners on the Active Contest Page

Once a weekly draw has been completed by the commercial team and winners are published, a dedicated **Les gagnants** (_Winners_) section appears directly on the contest page:

![Contest Page with Published Winners](../assets/public/contest-published-winners.png)
_Figure 4.11: The public contest page showcasing the winners section and winner count._

### Privacy-Preserving Winner Announcement

In compliance with Algerian privacy regulations, public winner names are automatically **masked** upon selection:

- Only the winner's first name and the first letter of their surname are made public (e.g., `Fatima Z.` or `Youcef M.`).
- Telephone numbers, email addresses, and receipt images are **strictly confidential** and never exposed to the public.

### The Winners Drawer / Modal

Clicking the button **Voir les gagnants** (_View winners_) expands the interactive drawer showcasing winners categorized by draw period:

![Public Winners Modal View](../assets/public/contest-winners-modal.png)
_Figure 4.12: The expanded winners list showing the draw period dates and published winners._

- Shows the period dates (e.g., _"Tirage du 18–24 octobre 2026"_).
- Lists each winning entrant in ranked order.
- Supports optional celebratory winner photos or video testimonials when provided with written participant consent.

---

## 4.3.2 Past Contests & The Public Archive

When a campaign reaches its scheduled conclusion or is marked as archived by staff, it transitions out of the active list and into the permanent **Concours passés** (_Past Contests_) archive (`/fr/concours/archives`):

![Past Contests Archive](../assets/public/archives-with-contest-fr.png)
_Figure 4.13: The public archive directory displaying concluded campaigns._

### Concluded Contest View

Visiting a past campaign directly shows its final historical state:

![Archived Contest View](../assets/public/contest-archived-view.png)
_Figure 4.14: An archived contest displaying its "Terminé" badge and historical winners._

- The badge displays **Terminé** (_Ended_).
- The participation form is removed and replaced by the closure notice: _"Ce concours est terminé. Merci à tous les participants !"_
- All published winners remain permanently verifiable for public transparency.

![Concluded Contest Closure Notice](../assets/public/contest-archived-closed-notice.png)
_Figure 4.15: Closed contest banner confirming participation has concluded._
