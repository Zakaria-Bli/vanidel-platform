# 4.1 Participant Experience: Browsing Contests

The public portal offers an intuitive, accessible experience designed for mobile devices, tablets, and desktop computers.

---

## 4.1.1 Discovering Active Campaigns

When consumers navigate to the platform (via a printed packaging QR code, influencer link, social ad, or direct URL), they land on the active contests page:

![Public Contests Browser - French](../assets/public/browse-contests-fr.png)
_Figure 4.1: Public contests listing in French._

### Elements of the Contests Directory

1. **Top Header**:
   - Vanidel official brand logo with direct link to home.
   - Navigation links: **Concours en cours** (_Active_) and **Concours passés** (_Past/Archives_).
   - Instant language switch toggle (**العربية** / **Français**).
2. **Hero Banner**:
   - Campaign headline: _"Participez et gagnez"_.
   - Value proposition: _"Achetez un produit Vanidel, gardez votre ticket de caisse et tentez votre chance."_
   - Official brand slogan badge: _"كلشي يسهال"_.
3. **Contest Cards**:
   - Status badge: **En cours** (_Active_).
   - Contest title (e.g., _"Concours Gamme Mix : tentez votre chance !"_).
   - Campaign date range (e.g. `25 oct. – 23 nov. 2026`).
   - Countdown timer (e.g. _"Plus que 28 jours"_).
   - Prize highlight (e.g. _"À gagner : 4 × Bon d’achat de 10 000 DA"_).
   - Call to action: **Participer** (_Enter now_).

## 4.1.3 Inspecting Contest Details

Clicking on a contest card opens its dedicated campaign page (e.g., `/fr/concours/gamme-mix-2026`):

![Public Contest Detail View](../assets/public/contest-details-fr.png)
_Figure 4.3: Dedicated contest landing page with visual assets and structured instructions._

The campaign landing page includes the following structured sections:

1. **Breadcrumb Navigation**: Link back to _Tous les concours_.
2. **Visual Showcase**: Full-width high-definition product visual (_Visuel principal_) showing qualifying products.
3. **Key Information Matrix (**L’essentiel**)**:
   - **Période**: Start and end dates.
   - **Tirage**: Draw cadence (e.g., _"Chaque semaine · 1 gagnant"_).
   - **Lots**: Total number of prizes up for grabs (e.g., _"4 lots à gagner"_).
   - **Participation**: Requirements and phone entry constraints (e.g., _"1 participation par numéro et par tirage"_).
4. **Description**: Promotional narrative introducing the campaign and brand context.
5. **How to Participate (**Comment participer**)**: Numbered, sequential steps guiding the consumer from buying the product and retaining their receipt to taking a clear photo.
6. **Prizes Showcase (**Lots à gagner**)**: Visual cards detailing prize descriptions and quantities.
7. **Direct Participation Anchor**: The button **Participer maintenant** scrolls smoothly to the participation form located at `#participer`.
